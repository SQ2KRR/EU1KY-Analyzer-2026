## V2.2 documentation

### Chapter 6 — S21 scalar transmission

The V2.2 S21 chapter describes:

- the meaning of S21 and scalar transmission measurements,
- four-port / two-port network terminology and S-parameters,
- S21 calibration and the 40 dB reference attenuator,
- measurement range, frequency step and sampling density,
- manual and Auto measurement modes,
- interpretation of all eight result pages,
- -3 dB bandwidth and additional -6 / -10 / -20 / -40 / -60 dB bandwidths,
- selectivity, shape factor, ripple and slope measurements,
- local maxima P1/P2 and the dip between them,
- practical measurements of LC, ceramic and quartz filters,
- the influence of source and load impedance,
- theory and history of ceramic and quartz filters.
